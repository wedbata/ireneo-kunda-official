About Ireneo Kunda Complex

Building a brighter future through quality education

Our Mission

To provide accessible, quality education that empowers students with knowledge, skills, and values needed to become responsible citizens and leaders in South Sudan and beyond.

Our Vision

To be the leading educational institution in Wau and Western Bahr el Ghazal, recognized for academic excellence, character development, and community impact.

Our Story

Ireneo Kunda Complex was established with a vision to transform education in Wau, South Sudan. Our school serves students from nursery through secondary levels, providing a comprehensive educational journey that prepares them for success in life.

Located in Wau, we are committed to serving our community by offering quality education that is both accessible and affordable. Our dedicated team of educators brings together experience, passion, and innovation to create a learning environment where every child can thrive.

Our Facilities

Ireneo Kunda Complex provides well-equipped facilities designed to support effective learning:

  • Well-stocked library with reading materials for all levels
  • Science laboratory for practical experiments
  • Computer lab for digital literacy
  • Spacious, well-ventilated classrooms
  • Sports and recreation grounds
  • Cafeteria providing nutritious meals
  • Health and first aid facilities
