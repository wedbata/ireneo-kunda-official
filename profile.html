School Leadership & Administration

Meet the visionary leaders and dedicated educators shaping tomorrow's leaders at Ireneo Kunda Complex

Executive Leadership

Guided by vision, integrity, and an enduring commitment to educational excellence in Wau, South Sudan.

Founder & Patron

Eng. Ireneo Kunda Tabour

Founder & Visionary Patron

Eng. Ireneo Kunda Tabour is the visionary founder and patron of Ireneo Kunda Complex. Driven by a lifelong commitment to community empowerment, socio-economic progress, and youth development in South Sudan, he established the complex to deliver holistic, high-quality education from early childhood through secondary graduation in Wau.

“Education is a key to success. Our vision is to equip every child in South Sudan with the knowledge, values, and resilience to build a prosperous tomorrow.”
Principal & Director

Dr. Cleto I. Kunda Tabour

Principal & Managing Director

Dr. Cleto I. Kunda Tabour leads the executive management, academic policy, and institutional operations of Ireneo Kunda Complex. Focused on educational rigor, modern instructional methodologies, and teacher excellence, he ensures that the school maintains an inspiring, safe, and academically competitive environment across all divisions.

“We believe in holistic education that nurtures the intellect, builds moral character, and prepares students to lead with confidence in a changing world.”

Head Teachers & Section Leaders

Experienced educational administrators leading our Nursery, Primary, and Secondary departments.

Secondary Level

Mr. Simon Luciano

Head Teacher — Secondary School

Mr. Simon Luciano leads the Secondary School division (Senior 1–4). He oversees curriculum implementation across the Science, Commercial, and Arts streams, preparing students for national examinations, STEM excellence, and university transition.

Key Focus Areas:

  • National Certificate Examination preparation
  • Science, Commercial & Arts stream supervision
  • Career guidance and university pathways
  • Academic discipline & leadership mentoring
Primary Level

Adiama Andrea

Head Teacher — Primary School

Adiama Andrea directs the Primary School section (Primary 1–8), emphasizing foundational literacy, numeracy, science, and character development. Under Andrea's leadership, pupils develop strong study habits and achieve top performance in the Primary Leaving Examination.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Primary 1–8 curriculum delivery & assessments
  • Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) excellence
  • Literacy, mathematics & science foundations
  • Co-curricular activities & student welfare
Nursery Level

Mrs. Janet Edward

Head Teacher — Nursery School

Mrs. Janet Edward manages our Early Childhood Education program (Ages 3–5). She specializes in child-centered, play-based methodologies that stimulate curiosity, early language acquisition, motor skills, and social development in a safe, warm environment.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Early childhood cognitive & emotional growth
  • Play-based learning & creative expression
  • Foundation literacy and numeracy skills
  • Child safety, health, and parent collaboration
