About Our School

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today

Ireneo Kunda Complex is a leading educational institution in Wau, South Sudan, dedicated to providing quality education from nursery through secondary levels. Our commitment is to nurture students' academic, social, and moral development in a supportive environment.

We believe in holistic education that prepares students not just for exams, but for life. Our experienced staff, modern teaching methods, and comprehensive curriculum ensure that each student reaches their full potential.
